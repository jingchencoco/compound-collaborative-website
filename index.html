01 / Ways of thinking and responding

lorem ipsum

Four projects trace how questions become observations, strategies, and practical design decisions.

China Promenade / Suxiangang Youth Park

What kind of life is produced when every need, movement, and choice has already been designed for us?

The proposal assembles borrowed forms and requested functions into a promenade that records—and quietly satirises—a controlled living environment.

 On Air

Can people occupy a site without also possessing its ground?

The buildings are lifted above a continuous forest floor, allowing human occupation while returning the ground to nature and wildlife.

 Architecture for Trees

What changes when trees, rather than people, become the primary clients?

Architecture becomes a restrained framework for conservation, observation, and coexistence within an ancient forest.

 Moscow Landscape Brainstorming 01

How can a landscape recover without erasing what happened?

Ruins hold the memory of war while walls, water, soil, and forest create the microclimates in which life can begin again.

About

COCO embraces friction, difference, and multiplicity, trusting that compounds of diverse practices will lead to more vital and enduring landscapes. We hold onto what is best for projects and for nature, allowing design to become not just form, but a collective and evolving way of expression.
