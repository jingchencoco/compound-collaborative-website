Cross Street 18&20

Location: Singapore
Status: Construction / 2022
Category: Offices
Non human client: -
Collaborator: BENOY

The project began during lockdown, when the absence of people made nature’s return newly visible.

Native wildflowers were placed at the upper reaches of the thirty-metre green facade, safely above the road and its constant disturbance.
