Architecture for Trees

Competition, 2021
Location: Guangzhou, China
Category: Designing for non human

A competition proposal that treats trees as the primary clients. Architecture becomes a framework for conservation, observation, and coexistence, forming a series of restrained interventions within an ancient forest landscape.
