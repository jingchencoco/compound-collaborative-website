Farmbyte Archisen

Location: Johor Bahru, Malaysia
Completed: 2024
Category: Offices
Collaborator: Farmbytes Archisen

Indoor agriculture offers a way to produce food without continually expanding farmland, leaving more forests intact and allowing damaged ecosystems to recover.

Yet it also risks a quieter loss: a generation growing distant from soil and forgetting the ancient relationship between food and land.
