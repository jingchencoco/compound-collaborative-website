Ice Plant

Category: Objects
The Curious Case of the Ice Plant

Ice plant is a remarkable competitor. As it dies, it returns salt to the soil, suppressing nearby plants and giving itself a natural advantage.

Its crystalline appearance—elegant and almost untouchable—became the starting point for the installation.
