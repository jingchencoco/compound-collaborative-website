WYZ House

Location: Malaysia
Status: Concept / 2024
Category: Houses
Collaborator: StudioKarya

WYZ House reads tropical living through the alphabet of the Malay house: anjung, windows within windows, rainwater jars, perching spots, shaded decks, and gardens that spill beyond rooms. The landscape does not decorate the house; it teaches the house how to live with rainforest memory, rural compounds, light, water, and everyday rituals.
