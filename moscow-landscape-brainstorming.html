Moscow Landscape
Brainstorming 01

Location: Moscow
Study: Landscape brainstorming
Project: Fictional

The project begins with the silence after war. Rammed-earth walls are made from chernozem soil; a chimney among the pine forest marks both human presence and the image of what remains after conflict. Ruins are not decoration, but spatial and historical evidence.

Glass bricks refer to Russia’s artificial-diamond industry and the trade altered by sanctions. The walls also work: they screen winter wind, absorb and release heat, and allow sunlight to reach the ground. Together they make small microclimates. A planted biopool filters water without chlorine and provides a source of life for wildlife.
